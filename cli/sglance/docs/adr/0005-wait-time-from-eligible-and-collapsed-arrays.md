# Measure wait time from eligible time with one sample per job array

Computing queue wait as `Start - Submit` across every started task row in `sacct` inflates partition wait percentiles whenever users hold jobs, chain dependencies, schedule future start times, or submit thousand-task throttled job arrays. `sglance` defines wait time as `Start - Eligible` for jobs started within the lookback window, collapses each job array to the single sample of its earliest started task, and excludes requeued records and jobs cancelled before starting. The reported `p50` and `p90` therefore measure scheduler contention once a job is runnable rather than total wall-clock time since `sbatch`, paired with the age of the oldest pending eligible job so active stalls remain visible before stalled jobs start.
