Coverage and method

A run is a stretch of rising readings in one window, ended by a reset or a gap. It stops at its first 100 % reading and is scaled to a whole window. The 10 largest rises of at least 20 % are averaged.

Codex logs a reading with every request. Claude Code logs only refusals and Antigravity logs nothing, so their figures come from live readings taken by usage-habit-report limits record.

Drift also prices every month at one reference model's rates, to separate a change in work from a change in prices. Means skip the current and first month. Requests paid with credits are left out.
